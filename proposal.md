## Analyses / visualizations / coding challenges

Potential challenges we may face in this analysis include aggregating
data from different publicly available NYC databases that may not have
properly corresponding variables or consistent formatting. Additionally,
using 311 complaint data to measure rat sightings may introduce
reporting bias, as the number of complaints may not accurately reflect
true rat populations. We may also encounter challenges when cleaning and
filtering large datasets, calculating rat sighting rates across
different time periods and boroughs, and creating Plotly visualizations
without performance issues. Another potential challenge would be
accounting for seasonal trends or other factors that may influence rat
sightings or populations will be important when comparing rates before
and after NYC’s trash bin policy in 2023.
